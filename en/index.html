About me

I am an entrepreneur first. With 99active I am building a local network backed by a digital platform. My consulting projects fund that venture, and they keep me close to how large organizations really work. What I try out in my own company I take into client projects, and the other way round. I don't tie myself to one role: depending on what the situation needs, I am an entrepreneur, a consultant or a coach.

I look at things the same way in my own company and with clients. I start with the people and what they need. Processes, organization and the numbers follow. And all of it has to fit the core business.

I started out in 1998 in the energy sector, in SAP billing at EnBW. Energy trading at E.ON, a bank merger at DVB, the digital subsidiary of the German Football Association (DFB) and a large cloud and platform transformation at adidas came later. Almost every one of these roles was about bringing order to processes that had grown over the years, getting business and IT around one table, and preparing decisions that hold. My training as an art and creative therapist shapes how I work with teams, especially when things get demanding.

What I work on

SAP and the energy sector

Eight years of hands-on SAP IS-U, then SAP from the controlling, finance and project lead side, through to S/4HANA Utilities and EEG Billing. I know billing, market communication and grid usage from the inside.

Tenders

I have prepared tenders for platforms, SAP and audit services and seen them through to completion. My part is turning business requirements into documents that vendors can answer in a comparable way.

Processes and organization

Mapping processes together with the people who do the work, then target pictures, roles and responsibilities. I use BPMN 2.0 and the usual modeling tools, but most of it happens in conversation.

Controlling and steering

IT budgets, forecasts, cost center and profit center structures, reporting for executives and steering committees. Numbers people understand are the basis for good decisions.

Teams and leadership

Leadership consulting for managers, team planning, recruiting and onboarding, support through change, and facilitation. At adidas I helped build a large platform team.

Go-to-market and our own systems

At 99active I lead the go-to-market strategy and build our own systems. Efficient documentation and automated processes keep manual work to a minimum. I bring that experience into client projects as well.

99active

99active

99active is what I am really building as an entrepreneur. We bring people together through local experiences: hosts offer workshops, tours and meetups, and participants book them on the platform. We first launched in 2021. What we learned led to 99active 2.0, which went live in February 2026.

My main role right now is a go-to-market strategy designed to scale. We build the network first and develop the platform around what people actually need. Alongside that I build our own systems, such as the 99active CRM, so that much of the work runs automatically in the background and there is time for people up front.
